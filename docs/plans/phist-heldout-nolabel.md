# P-hist: candidate held-out evaluation without full transcription

Status: proposal, 2026-11-20. No evaluation, reference construction, scan download or OCR run was performed. No candidate is registered or cleared of conflicts. The released old-print model has **not been measured on a held-out set yet** by this proposal.

## What can be measured

Existing digital editions can cheaply locate passages on scans, but they are not necessarily exact transcriptions of the images. Their discrepancies include edition changes, skipped commentaries, spelling, marks and transcription errors. A score against unverified text confounds these with OCR errors. Under [decision 0012](../../planning/decisions/0012-real-page-scored-sets.md), silver references are development signals, not benchmark results; simply calling a silver set held out does not change that. Nor should protected test pages be exposed as development signals. A genuine real-scan benchmark needs human-adjudicated image-matched references or a new maintainer decision. The economical compromise is to adjudicate a **small, randomly sampled, fixed subset**, not fully transcribe all books. The official 22-book test remains separate and blocked until its own references are ready.

## Registration and alignment protocol

1. Lead checks every full work, edition, scan and text against the private reservation list and all existing training, validation, pretraining, glyph banks and model initialization, including alternate editions and near duplicates. A previously contaminated work is ineligible; do not try to repair it by holding out different pages. Reserve accepted whole works and their derivatives against **all future training** before any construction. Record source IDs, exact revisions, licences and hashes in a private manifest; do not expose protected pages to workers. If origin or rights cannot be established, reject the item.
2. Before test inspection, freeze eligibility, stratified random sampling, region scope, matching parameters and failure handling. Obtain a separate non-test development source for implementing alignment. Later an authorized evaluation custodian uses existing scan text or a frozen independent alignment recognizer, locates verse/section anchors, then performs monotonic fuzzy matching over local page windows; use confidence and margin against alternative matches, detect repeats, omissions and reordered pages. Preserve raw text, original Unicode, page ID and coordinates. An OCR-assisted aligner may select easy text: record all pages attempted, failures and rejection reasons; do not report scores conditional on easy matches alone. No evaluation OCR is authorized by this plan.
3. Use a blind random audit across books, script, quality and match-confidence bands, including failures; independent human comparison to the scan checks complete selected regions, page boundaries, order, omissions and punctuation, with adjudication. Fix references to the image, not vice versa. Prespecify sample size and acceptable reference error, estimate uncertainty from the audit, and stop if it fails; selective spotting alone does not establish gold. For partial regions report coverage and exclusions explicitly; full-page metrics require complete page references and reading order. A small adjudicated subset can become gold after registration, while the remainder is unscored silver.
4. Freeze model, scorer, segmentation, normalization and all thresholds before the custodian scores once. Follow [decision 0017](../../planning/decisions/0017-domain-separated-evaluation.md): CER, WER, line exact rate and CER, page CER median/p90 and fixed-threshold shares, plus reading-order/layout cost where references support them; stratify square/Rashi/mixed and pointed/unpointed. Cluster intervals by independent source/edition, not page; this one MG edition cannot supply reliable edition-level generalization. Report denominators and coverage. No reported result is promised until a registered set and suitable references exist.

## Generated old-print held-out set

This is a **generated-data measurement**: exact labels describe rendered text, but pages are not real documents and performance does not establish old-scan accuracy. This is the same synthetic-gold caveat as [the baseline](../baselines.md#first-results-synthetic-gold-2026-11-01): exact generated references do not make generated pages real. The original 22-book test remains necessary for real-scan claims.

Use public-domain ancient texts from a separately licensed, pinned transcription, such as a verified public-domain edition, and record the exact source and licence. Ensure source passages and near duplicates are disjoint from training and all real-test passages; use different works if that cannot be established. Reserve text, layout variants, seeds and **entire font families** before rendering. Include square, Rashi and mixed commentary layouts, multiple sizes, columns and abbreviations, with correct logical RTL labels, line/region boxes and reading order. Validate shaping, marks, fallback and missing glyphs on separate development inputs. Freeze independent train/dev/test seeds and text before rendering; generate gold from renderer input, not OCR.

[hocrsyngen at 6632c6c](https://github.com/HeOCR/hocrsyngen/tree/6632c6cfc7a33e93fe89ac9de8fc8bc181019a4f) lists Alef, Frank Ruhl Libre, David Libre, Noto Rashi and others in its [font manifest](https://github.com/HeOCR/hocrsyngen/blob/6632c6cfc7a33e93fe89ac9de8fc8bc181019a4f/src/hocrsyngen/data/synthetic/fonts/manifest.yaml); these are **not safe unseen candidates**. Its [exclusion list](https://github.com/HeOCR/hocrsyngen/blob/6632c6cfc7a33e93fe89ac9de8fc8bc181019a4f/src/hocrsyngen/data/fonts_excluded.json) reserves Drugulin CLM and Simple CLM for a different test; [the guard](https://github.com/HeOCR/hocrsyngen/blob/6632c6cfc7a33e93fe89ac9de8fc8bc181019a4f/src/hocrsyngen/font_exclusion.py) checks family, filename and hash, with an eval-only override. This is not proof of a model's complete font history. Commentary font may be absent from older page manifests; inspect generator recipes too.

Candidate Rashi face: [Mekorot](https://github.com/googlefonts/mekorot), [SIL OFL 1.1](https://github.com/googlefonts/mekorot/blob/main/OFL.txt). Candidate square face: [Frank Ruhl Hofshi](https://github.com/emmamarichal/frankruhlhofshi), [SIL OFL 1.1](https://github.com/emmamarichal/frankruhlhofshi/blob/master/OFL.txt); related to Frank Ruhl designs already used, so font-family and outline lineage leakage is a serious concern. Both are **proposals only**, not proven unseen. Check all HeOCR and upstream Tesseract training inventories, aliases, derivatives and outline similarity; reject on uncertainty. Do not reuse the unrelated niqqud test's reserved fonts without the lead's coordination. Pin source commit, binaries, license and hashes; extend the training guard to block accepted families, aliases and all weights, audit every renderer and fallback path, and keep eval override isolated. If clean model provenance cannot be demonstrated, label font novelty unverified and do not claim unseen fonts.

Render a clean control and independently varied, bounded old-print effects: uneven ink and broken strokes, bleed-through, paper yellowing and stains, foxing, warped gutter, skew, blur, uneven illumination, downsampling and compression. Avoid hiding characters or changing labels; record parameters and stratify by severity. Preregister page counts, seed, recipe, model hashes, scoring profiles and cluster-level uncertainty; evaluate only after the lead registers the set and freezes the model. Validate generated gold and scan realism against non-test sources; do not tune on held-out outputs. No computation is requested in this PR.
