# Hebrew OCR and HTR comparator inventory

Surveyed 2026-11-20 from public documentation; no system was run and no evaluation images, accounts or paid services were used. [comparators.csv](comparators.csv) is the candidate-level register, including sources, version, scope, execution, licensing, publication status and costs. `Unknown` means not established, not prohibited. A provider's model card or benchmark is evidence of a candidate, not proof that it is strongest. No outside score is adopted as a HeOCR result.

## Proposed category sets

These are **proposals for registration**, not an assertion that the systems are cleared or have been beaten. Include every listed applicable candidate in the claim once eligibility, reproducibility and rights are resolved; do not silently drop a difficult system. The slash separates open/local candidates from hosted candidates, which remain conditional on access, terms, data transfer rights and funding. For a broad state-of-the-art claim include both strata and the separate LLM stratum; otherwise qualify the claim to the registered stratum and category. A missing, ineligible, unrun or unpublishable strong comparator prevents an unqualified claim.

Add Gemini, GPT vision and Claude vision as a **separate LLM-reader stratum** for each applicable category, with exact model snapshot, prompt, sampling, image resolution and output limit fixed before measurement. General-purpose models may omit or invent text. A family name alone is not a reproducible system. Commercial or restrictive systems can still be important comparators, but the charter's open-source rule and the actual licence must be resolved before their use; inclusion here does not authorize downloading them or submitting data.

## Registration and scoring protocol

1. Before opening any evaluation material, the lead selects the category's real, registered, held-out set and fixes eligible comparators, model and segmenter versions and SHA-256, software, runtime, hardware, settings, preprocessing, page/line input and output extraction. Record any training or selection overlap with the set; exclude contaminated comparisons or report them separately. A line-only recognizer needs a fixed, disclosed segmentation pipeline; it is not equivalent to an end-to-end page system.
2. Fix text scope, reading order, Unicode normalization, niqqud and cantillation policy, punctuation, missing lines, blank outputs and failures before the run. Apply the identical evaluator-side policy to all predictions. Score omissions and insertions; do not drop failed pages. Pointed and unpointed profiles must not erase the distinction under test. Report full coverage and uncertainty on paired comparisons.
3. The rule is `CER_HeOCR <= 0.95 * CER_comparator` for **every** registered comparator on the same real held-out set and profile. A 5% relative reduction is not five percentage points. Prespecify treatment of zero CER, confidence intervals and significance; no numerical or state-of-the-art claim follows from this inventory.
4. Confirm permissions to send the exact images and retain and publish derived outputs and aggregate scores, under the actual account contract and applicable model version. `unknown` in the CSV blocks a public claim until resolved. Record the agreement, date, section and any written permission. Pricing is indicative and region, tier and date dependent. Scoring outputs locally does not imply permission to publish them or their scores.

## Evidence and unresolved rights

* Tesseract [best](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best) and [fast](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast) distribute Apache-2.0 traineddata; the [engine](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract) is Apache-2.0. Pin separate `heb.traineddata` hashes. Rivok's [2.0 model card](https://huggingface.co/Rivok/tesseract-heb-rivok) explicitly lists niqqud and old print and supplies vendor-reported results. Its [Paddle model card](https://huggingface.co/Rivok/paddleocr-hebrew) identifies ONNX Apache-2.0 models and vendor-reported test results; inspect each selected file's lineage.
* [BiblIA general](https://zenodo.org/records/5468286) is CC BY-SA 4.0; the [Sofer Mahir repository](https://github.com/dstoekl/sofer_mahir) links Ashkenazi, Italian and Sephardi recognition models and segmentation models, whose individual licences must be checked. [Decision 0006](../planning/decisions/0006-model-licences.md) leaves share-alike's application to predictions unresolved. The [PP-OCRv6 small record](https://zenodo.org/records/21788405) identifies Hebrew in a multilingual print/handwriting line model; the record states Apache-2.0; check its exact version, runtime branch and selected variant. Its listing confuses small and medium, so do not assume which variant is strongest. Search the [OCR/HTR model repository](https://zenodo.org/communities/ocr_models) and [HTR-United catalog](https://htr-united.github.io/) for more matching releases before preregistration. Tikkoun Sofrim is a project, not an identified, independently downloadable recognition model in this survey; do not substitute a name for a model ID.
* [Mishkefet](https://huggingface.co/itayinbar/Mishkefet-v1) is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, its code MIT; its authors report modern handwriting results and disclose possible page-segmenter exposure to benchmark pages. [cyttic's ONNX card](https://huggingface.co/cyttic/hebrew-ocr-android) labels its bundle MIT, but the full pipeline and training lineage need verification. [Tzefa](https://huggingface.co/WARAJA/Tzefa-Word-OCR-TrOCR) recognizes a constrained programming vocabulary, not general handwriting. Donut and Nougat are architectures/checkpoints, not verified Hebrew-specific comparators here. Stock [EasyOCR](https://github.com/JaidedAI/EasyOCR/issues/363) has no established Hebrew recognizer; stock docTR and PaddleOCR must not be conflated with independently fine-tuned Hebrew weights.
* [Surya](https://github.com/datalab-to/surya) currently describes Apache-2.0 code and modified AI Pubs Open RAIL-M weights with use restrictions; licences differ by release. [Decision 0021](../planning/decisions/0021-third-party-engines-in-evaluation.md) permits a specific local research-use route; check its pinned version and terms, and whether the charter permits the chosen source. No hosted API is authorized by that decision.
* [Dicta](https://dicta.org.il/tools?lang=en) lists OCR and Rashi OCR; identify the exact service and get its terms. [ivrit.ai](https://github.com/ivrit-ai/ivrit-handwriting-collection) has a handwriting collection and benchmark, not an independently identified deployable reader in this survey. The [NLI-associated pipeline](https://github.com/omilab/2022_OCR_Pipeline) describes access to selected newspaper OCR outputs, not a generally available standalone NLI reader. Contact maintainers only through the lead if access or permission is needed.
* [AWS language support](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/textract/latest/dg/supported-languages.html) includes Hebrew for `DetectDocumentText`; its [FAQ](https://aws.amazon.com/textract/faqs/) limits handwriting to English. The [Azure language table](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/document-intelligence/language-support-ocr) must be checked for the exact API version and print/handwriting mode. [Google processor documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/document-ai/docs/processors-list) and [Vision language support](https://cloud.google.com/vision/docs/languages) need the same mode-specific check. Neither niqqud nor Rashi support is established by a generic Hebrew label.
* Google Cloud's [service terms](https://cloud.google.com/terms/service-terms) contain benchmarking conditions; historical versions differ, including on written consent. The currently published section 7 allows the customer itself to benchmark, but does not permit a third party to do so; public disclosure requires information necessary to replicate and reciprocal benchmarking rights. Testing or disclosure on behalf of a hyperscale public cloud provider requires prior written consent. Check the actual contract and version before use. Other hosted systems' precise disclosure and written-permission terms were not verified. They remain conditional, not presumed allowed. Price sources: [Vision](https://cloud.google.com/vision/pricing), [Document AI](https://cloud.google.com/products/document-ai/pricing), [Textract](https://aws.amazon.com/textract/pricing/); other prices require an applicable current quote.

Published Hebrew results, where reported by vendors or maintainers, are linked in the CSV and above for discovery only; datasets, splits, normalization and possible contamination are not verified and they are not HeOCR results. Sources were consulted as public documentation, not downloaded models or evaluation data. All links and prices are time-sensitive.
